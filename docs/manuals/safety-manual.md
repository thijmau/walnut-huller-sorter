# Safety Manual

> [!WARNING]
> **Not certified!** This machine does not possess any CE, UKCA, FC or UL
assessment, nor is it planned as of now. Whoever builds this machine is its
manufacturer and is responsible for its safety and compliance, including a risk
assessment, guarding, an emergency stop and the electrical installation.

## Before use

- Read this page in its entirety
- Check that all guards are in place and that the emergency stop works
  accordingly
- Check that the distribution- and control panel are closed and dry
- Only the operator controls this machine; helpers only load the hopper. Keep
  children away.

## Intended use

This machine hulls and sorts walnuts, specifically made for
the [Juglans Regia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juglans_regia), it may work on
other walnuts, although it was not developed for those. The machine is supposed
to be controlled by a **single operator**; although other people or devices may
help loading walnuts into the hopper.

The machine only starts with the **START** button, which is implemented with a
soft-start mechanism to prevent jolting. It can stop by pressing the **STOP**
button, after which the machine can easily be restarted by pressing the
**START** button again.

It is also possible to put the machine to a halt by turning it off using the
**MAIN ROTARY SWITCH**, although after this the **START** button needs to be
pressed again upon reactivating.

Finally, in case of an emergency, the **E-STOP** button can be pressed. This
mechanism isolates the conductor (s) completely. After re-activating the button,
the machine does not turn on. Before the **MAIN ROTARY SWITCH** and the
**START** button would even work again, the **RESET** button needs to be pressed
to reactivate the conductor (s) to prevent the machine turning on by itself
after reactivating the **E-STOP**.

## During use

- Never reach into the drum, hopper, rollers, belt and especially the fan while
  the machine is running.
- Do not spray water at the electrical enclosure (or the machine in general).
- Do not drink from the water supply, it is a contaminated recirculating loop.
- Stop the machine with the emergency stop when anything goes wrong.

## Maintenance/cleaning

1. Disconnect the machine from mains before maintenance or cleaning. **Stopping
   it is never enough**.
2. Wait until the drum, fan and other rotating parts have **come to a complete
   stop**.
3. Wash the entire machine by hand, **do not** hose it down or use a pressure
   washer.
4. Take the drum and/or endcaps off for cleaning **only after** the steps above.

## Hazards

This machine contains several hazards which could either be a mild inconvenience
or seriously injure a person. The table below describes in detail what hazards the
machine contains, their locations and the risks involved if ignored.

| Hazard                    | Where                                                              | What can happen                                                                                 |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Crushing and entanglement | Rollers on the end-cap rims; belt onto the pulley and motor pinion | Fingers, hair, gloves or loose clothing are pulled in and crushed                               |
| Caught by the drum        | Input hopper, which opens into the rotating drum                   | A hand reaching in is caught                                                                    |
| (Deep) Cuts               | Fan                                                                | The blades cut fingers that reach into the fan                                                  |
| Noise                     | Fan, tumbling drum, coaster wheels                                 | Long exposure damages hearing                                                                   |
| Pinching                  | Dump flap: which moves on its own                                  | A hand between the flap and its frame is pinched                                                |
| Cuts and abrasion         | Inside the drum: abrasive wall, sharp-edged perforations           | Bare hands are scraped or cut                                                                   |
| Electric shock            | Electrical enclosure: Mains                                        | Touching live parts, with the enclosure open or through damaged wiring; **Mains can be lethal** |
| Water on electrical parts | Electrical enclosure                                               | Wet parts become live or short-circuit: shock, damaged electronics or fire                      |
| Unsafe water              | Water supply, recirculating through the machine                    | Illness from drinking it                                                                        |
| Walnut husks              | Husk juice                                                         | Skin stains dark brown for days and can become irritated                                        |

The stickers, their codes and locations are listed
in the [safety stickers](../stickers/README.md) document.
